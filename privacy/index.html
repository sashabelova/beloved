What information we collect, use, and why

When you sign up to our newsletter, we collect:

  • Your email address
  • A record of when you signed up and agreed to receive emails from us
  • Whether you open our emails and which links in them you click

We use this only to send you news about our screenings and upcoming events, and to see which news is of most interest. Brevo records email opens and clicks for us automatically; you can stop this at any time by unsubscribing.

Event bookings and ticket purchases

When you purchase tickets for events organised by Beloved Films Ltd through Ticket Tailor, information such as your name, email address and booking details is collected to process and manage your order.

We may access this information through Ticket Tailor to:

  • Manage bookings and ticket purchases.
  • Send essential information about events you have booked.
  • Handle cancellations, refunds and customer enquiries.
  • Maintain necessary accounting and financial records.

We process booking information where necessary to fulfil our contract with you and to comply with applicable legal obligations.

Marketing emails are entirely optional. Purchasing a ticket does not automatically subscribe you to our mailing list.

If you explicitly choose to receive marketing emails during checkout, we may transfer your name, email address and consent information to Brevo, our email marketing provider, to send you updates about upcoming screenings, ticket releases and special events.

If you do not opt in, we will not add you to our marketing mailing list on the basis of that ticket purchase. You can withdraw your marketing consent at any time by using the unsubscribe link in our emails.

Payment processing

We use Stripe to securely process ticket payments. Payment card details are provided directly to Stripe, and we do not store full card details ourselves.

Stripe processes payment information in accordance with its own privacy policy.

Lawful bases and data protection rights

Under UK data protection law, we must have a “lawful basis” for collecting and using your personal information. There is a list of possible lawful bases in the UK GDPR. You can find out more about lawful bases on the ICO’s website.

Which lawful basis we rely on may affect your data protection rights which are set out in brief below. You can find out more about your data protection rights and the exemptions which may apply on the ICO’s website:

  • Your right of access - You have the right to ask us for copies of your personal information. You can request other information such as details about where we get personal information from and who we share personal information with. There are some exemptions which means you may not receive all the information you ask for.
  • Your right to rectification - You have the right to ask us to correct or delete personal information you think is inaccurate or incomplete.
  • Your right to erasure - You have the right to ask us to delete your personal information.
  • Your right to restriction of processing - You have the right to ask us to limit how we can use your personal information.
  • Your right to object to processing - You have the right to object to the processing of your personal data.
  • Your right to data portability - You have the right to ask that we transfer the personal information you gave us to another organisation, or to you.
  • Your right to withdraw consent - When we use consent as our lawful basis you have the right to withdraw your consent at any time.

If you make a request, we must respond to you without undue delay and in any event within one month.

To make a data protection rights request, please contact us using the contact details at the top of this privacy notice.

Our lawful bases for the collection and use of your data

Our lawful bases for collecting and using your personal information are:

  • Consent - for sending you our newsletter and other marketing emails, including if you opt in at checkout. We have permission from you after we gave you all the relevant information. All of your data protection rights may apply, except the right to object. To be clear, you do have the right to withdraw your consent at any time, by using the unsubscribe link in any of our emails or by emailing us.
  • Contract - for processing your booking and ticket purchase. All of your data protection rights may apply, except the right to object.
  • Legal obligation - for keeping the booking and payment records we must hold for accounting and tax purposes. All of your data protection rights may apply, except the right to erasure, the right to object and the right to data portability.

Where we get personal information from

We collect your personal information directly from you: when you subscribe to our newsletter on our website, and when you buy tickets through Ticket Tailor, including if you choose to receive marketing emails at checkout.

Who we share information with

We use Brevo to store our mailing list and send our newsletter. Brevo processes your information only on our instructions. Brevo is based in France and stores data in the European Union.

We do not sell your personal information or share it with anyone else for marketing.

We use Ticket Tailor to manage ticket bookings and attendee information for our events. Ticket Tailor processes this information on our behalf to provide its ticketing services. You can find more information about Ticket Tailor at tickettailor.com.

We use Stripe to process ticket payments securely. Stripe processes payment information in accordance with its privacy policy.

Embedded content and cookies

Our website does not use analytics or advertising cookies.

Some pages show content from other websites: Instagram videos on the Film Club page, and a video hosted on Google Drive on the Past Events page. These behave as if you had visited Instagram or Google directly, and Meta and Google may collect information about you and set cookies, under their own privacy policies.

Our home page stores a small note in your browser so the intro animation is only shown once a week. It stays on your device and is not sent to us.

Links to other websites

Our website links to other sites, such as Ticket Tailor and Instagram. This notice does not cover them, so please read their own privacy policies.

How long we keep information

We retain newsletter subscription information while you remain subscribed. If you unsubscribe, we may retain a limited record of your preference to ensure we do not send you further marketing emails.

We retain booking information for as long as necessary to manage events, handle customer enquiries and meet applicable legal obligations.

Financial and accounting records are generally retained for six years from the end of the relevant financial year, or longer where required by law.

Our ticketing and payment service providers may retain information for different periods in accordance with their applicable privacy notices and legal obligations.
