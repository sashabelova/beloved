About the screening

Edgar Wright's 2010 comedy stars Michael Cera as Scott Pilgrim, a young bassist who has to defeat the seven evil exes of Mary Elizabeth Winstead's Ramona Flowers before he can date her. It's iconic, funny and just simply charming.

Beloved Film Club is our regular film night in a relaxed pub setting, celebrating great cinema. Come on your own or bring a friend: everyone is welcome. The film starts at 7:00 PM, and we recommend arriving earlier to grab a drink or something to eat before getting comfortable. Popcorn on us 🍿

About Beloved Film Club

Beloved Film Club is a regular community film night run by Beloved Films. We show independent films, cult classics and favourites worth rewatching, for people who love movies and for anyone curious to discover something new.
